This page explains what Infinite Laboratory stores about you and why. We keep it short and we collect as little as we can.

Today this website stores nothing about you: there are no accounts yet. This page describes what we will collect when they open.

## What we collect

- **Minecraft:** your Minecraft UUID and name, your fellow number, and basic play data such as playtime and the stats shown on leaderboards.
- **Discord login:** if you create a website account, your Discord user ID, display name and the email Discord gives us.
- **Profile:** the username, bio and website link you choose to add.
- **Requests:** if you ask to use our brand, the name, email and details you send. We keep them only to answer you.
- **Technical:** your IP address and device information in security logs, and the sessions you are logged in on.

We do not collect passwords. We do not store your Discord password or tokens.

## Why we collect it

To run the server, give you a permanent fellow number, let you log in and link your player, show leaderboards, keep the community safe, and answer reports and requests.

## What other people can see

Your username, role, bio and fellow number can be public. Your in-game name, Discord name, email and whether you linked an account are not shown to others. Players see your Minecraft name in the game, as on any server.

## Who we share it with

We use Discord for login, a database host for storing accounts, and a skin image service for faces. We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete your account. Security logs are kept for a limited time, then deleted.

## Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your data. Deleting your account removes your website account and links. Your fellow number is retired and never reused. Ask in Discord #support or from your account page. Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you can also contact us to use your rights.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in. We use no advertising cookies.

## Contact

Questions about your data: join our Discord and open a thread in #support.
