Armored SMP was a whitelist survival server where armor could not be crafted. Every death gave you armor that levelled up from leather, and full netherite meant a 12-hour ban.

## What happened

It ran for three months, then went offline for good. Players left when no admin was around to play with them.

## What we changed

Technicians play in the world, and the world has to run itself. That is the idea behind Deepslate MC.
