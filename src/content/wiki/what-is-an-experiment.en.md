Infinite Laboratory is a Minecraft studio. Each server it runs is an **experiment** with its own name and an `EXP` number.

## The experiments so far

- **EXP-001, Armored SMP.** Our first server. It is archived. Read [what we learned](armored-smp-lessons).
- **EXP-002, Deepslate MC.** A jobs economy on Java and Bedrock. It is in development.

## Why experiments

Rules and mechanics can change on purpose. Players are fellows and testers: your feedback shapes what changes, and it shows up in the [build log](../build-log).
