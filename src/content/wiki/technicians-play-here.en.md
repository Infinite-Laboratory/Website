Technicians are the studio's staff. They log in to **play**, not only to moderate, and they follow the same rules as everyone.

## Why it matters

Our first server, Armored SMP, went offline for good when players left because no admin was around to play with them. Deepslate MC is built to run itself, and Technicians are in the world with you.

## How to reach them

Open a thread in Discord `#support`. If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal it there. See the [rules](../rules) for how decisions work.
