## Be a good fellow

Treat every player as a fellow tester. No harassment, slurs, or targeting anyone for who they are.

## No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray, macros or auto-clickers. Report it; do not retaliate.

## Break the experiment, then tell us

Found a bug? Report it with /feedback or in Discord #feedback. Using a bug for gain without reporting it is not testing.

## Technicians are players

Technicians play under the same rules as everyone. They also moderate, and their decisions can be appealed.

## Keep the economy honest

No duping, no real-money trading, no account selling. The economy is the experiment.

## One account, one fellow

Your fellow number is permanent and belongs to you. Do not share or sell your account.

## Keep chat readable

No spam, no advertising other servers, no flooding. Thai and English are both welcome.

## Build without griefing

Do not destroy or steal from other players' builds. Claim your land and respect claims.

## No exploiting lag or crashes

Do not intentionally crash or slow the server. Tell us how you found it instead.

## Respect privacy

Do not share another player's personal information, in game or outside it.
